## OmniFind 0.3.0-beta.3 · Build 6

Beta · 2026-10-20 · Android 9 (API 28) to Android 17 (API 37) · arm64

Folders refresh on demand and system folders are protected; a tour shows what's new after every install or update, with screenshots and results from a real run on a Pixel 8 Pro emulator. Includes beta.2's fixes for Android 15–17, every screen density and large text.

### What's in this build

1. **Search by what's in your files** (Feature). Describe what you're looking for in your own words. Exact words, names and codes are found at once; meaning fills in a moment later, across languages.
2. **Photos and screenshots by what they show** (Feature). Pictures are searched by what's in them, not their names. The Pictures filter shows matches as a grid.
3. **Choose what to index** (Feature). Tick documents, PDFs and pictures. Files already indexed and unchanged are skipped, and nothing is embedded twice.
4. **Refresh folders, system folders protected** (New in this build). Tap Refresh under Phone storage or SD card to list folders made since; new ones are marked New. Android's system folders and other apps' files are never shown or read.
5. **Download the model in the app** (Feature). Search by meaning needs a one-time 865 MB download over Wi-Fi. It resumes if interrupted and is checked before use; nothing about your files is sent.
6. **Fits every screen** (Fixed in beta.2). Phones from small ldpi screens to the Pixel 8 Pro, tablets and landscape, with large text: nothing overlaps or is cut off, and the app runs edge to edge on Android 15 to 17.
7. **This tour, after every update** (New in this build). The tour opens once after you install or update OmniFind. Open it again any time from Engine → What's new.

### Fixed

1. Content no longer runs under the status and navigation bars on Android 15–17 _(0.3.0-beta.2)_
2. The privacy note no longer covers the examples on short screens and in landscape _(0.3.0-beta.2)_
3. Buttons, tags and chips no longer cut words off at large text sizes or on 320 dp screens _(0.3.0-beta.2)_
4. Thumbnails are sharp on every density (they asked for a fixed pixel size) _(0.3.0-beta.2)_
5. Launcher icon in every density bucket, round and themed (Android 13+) versions _(0.3.0-beta.2)_
6. "All files access" on Android 11+ and notification permission on 13+ _(0.3.0-beta.2)_
7. A folder reached through a link, or a path back into Android/, is refused _(0.3.0-beta.3)_
8. The caution before indexing says PDFs, not pdfs _(0.3.0-beta.3)_

### Known issues

- Scanned PDFs (pages without text) are not searchable yet: planned for milestone 4.
- Text inside screenshots isn't searchable by its exact words; screenshots are found by what they show.
- arm64 phones only. On newer CPUs (Pixel 8 Pro) the engine doesn't yet use their faster instructions.
